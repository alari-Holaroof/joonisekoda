Installation manuals the installer actually follows.

Step by step: which parts, where they go, how they connect and what fastens them. Every step is drawn from the 3D model, so the parts and numbers match the production drawings — and the manual ends with a safety zone plan where the product needs one.

What you get

Connections and fasteners

How it works

01 · Brief

You send a 3D model, production drawings or photos of the product with dimensions.

02 · Model

We split the assembly into steps, check the order and agree on the layout and language.

03 · Delivery

You get a print-ready PDF manual. Updates are easy, because the steps come from the model.

Frequently asked questions

What do you need to make a manual?

A 3D model is best. Production drawings or photos with dimensions also work — then we model the product first.

Which languages?

We work in English and Estonian. Because manuals are mostly pictures, another language means translating the captions, not redrawing the steps.

Can the manual include a safety zone?

Yes. For playground equipment and similar products we add a safety zone plan showing the clear area around the product.

What happens when the product changes?

The steps are made from the model, so after a change in the model the affected pages are updated instead of redrawn.
