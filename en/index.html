Why Joonisekoda?

One model.
One source of truth.

When design, production, installation and training all work from different files, mistakes follow. Our aim is to keep the whole body of technical information around one logical model — from the drawing all the way to the animation and the training game.

  • ✓ Less rework in production
  • ✓ Fewer questions on the installation site
  • ✓ More accurate material consumption
  • ✓ The same part number on the drawing, in the manual and in the game
